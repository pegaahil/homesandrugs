Reflection

The source-to-claim step needed the most editorial attention in this run: attribution, conflicting dates and naming scope. This is a qualitative observation from one run, not a measured long-term failure rate. Source screening now precedes illustration selection and translation. The process remains a loop because a slide wording problem must be corrected in the Wiki, not only on the slide.

Image permission review also became an early gate. A public article alone is insufficient permission to copy its photographs. Remaining limits: Chinese human review, no specialist authentication of unknown rugs, and no quantitative valuation model.
