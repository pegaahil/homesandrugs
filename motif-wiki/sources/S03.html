Source note S03

The Metropolitan Museum of Art. “Floral Arabesque Carpet,” 17th century, probably Iran. Rogers Fund, 1969, accession 69.244.

Open public source

Accessed 2026-11-24 · Museum object record

Credibility

Collection attribution, material record and open image.

Limitations

Preserve “probably Iran”; the record discusses earlier Iran/India attributions. This one object does not define all arabesques.

Evidence notes

Excerpt IDs identify our concise paraphrase notes, not verbatim quotations. Read the linked original for context.

S03-E1

The museum attributes this carpet to probably Iran and records wool pile with cotton foundation. Blue arabesque bands share the field with smaller floral elements.

S03-E2

The public-domain image shows broad blue curving bands and smaller red-ground flowers. These observable shapes do not identify the intention of the weaver.
