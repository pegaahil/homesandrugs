Source note S01

Ittig, Annette. “CARPETS iv. Knotted-pile carpets: Designs, motifs, and patterns.” Encyclopaedia Iranica IV/8 (1990), 845–850. Updated 2017.

Open public source

Accessed 2026-11-24 · Specialist reference

Credibility

Named specialist, terminology and bibliography.

Limitations

Historical overview; nomenclature varies. Its Ardabil date differs from the current V&A record; do not copy that date.

Evidence notes

Excerpt IDs identify our concise paraphrase notes, not verbatim quotations. Read the linked original for context.

S01-E1

Field is enclosed by main and minor border stripes, separated by narrow guards. Motif, pattern and overall design are different levels.

Locator in original: Designs; opening terminology

S01-E2

Herati combines a rosette, diamond, palmettes and four curved leaves. Mahi refers to the leaves’ fish-like appearance, not necessarily literal fish.

Locator in original: Patterns, item 1: Herati

S01-E3

Arabesque uses vegetal scrolling and divided leaves. Palmettes are stylized fan-like flowers.

Locator in original: Motifs, items 3 and 5

S01-E4

Shah Abbasi can name a repeat of floral elements, palmettes and cloud bands. It is not a secure date or maker attribution.

Locator in original: Patterns: Shah Abbasi

S01-E5

Medallion-and-corner design combines a central toranj with corner portions. Terminology is not uniform.

Locator in original: Designs, item 1
