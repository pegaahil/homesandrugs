Source note S07

Cleveland Museum of Art. “Carpet Fragment,” accession 1988.243, Gift of Milton Girod. Object record and full conservation statement.

Open public source

Accessed 2026-11-24 · Museum record and conservation report

Credibility

Reports 2019 scientific work and cites the 2021 forensic study.

Limitations

Current record says Iran, Rayy. Natural versus synthetic indigo remains unresolved. Provenance includes question marks.

Evidence notes

Excerpt IDs identify our concise paraphrase notes, not verbatim quotations. Read the linked original for context.

S07-E1

The conservation statement dates the fragment to 1884–1928, following dye analysis. Congo Red provides a late nineteenth-century boundary. This revises older claims of great antiquity.

Locator in original: Full conservation statement, Dye analysis

S07-E2

The museum could not determine whether the indigo was natural or synthetic. The object is silk knotted pile, with palmettes and a central medallion.

Locator in original: Dye analysis and Description

S07-E3

Its ownership history contains uncertain dates and a questioned transfer. Such uncertainty should remain visible in a provenance summary.
