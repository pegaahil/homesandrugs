What makes a rug valuable?

A documentation guide for collectors, not an appraisal.

Materials

Historical claimRecord the fibre of pile and foundation separately. A silk label alone is not a monetary valuation.
S05-E1S08-E1

Knot density

Historical claimRecord a unit and method. Density can enable detail, but the Wiki supplies no price conversion or universal quality ranking.
S06-E1S04-E3

Dyes

Historical claimDocument testing where available. Screen colour cannot establish dye chemistry. Natural versus synthetic is not a simple value scale.
S05-E1S07-E2

Condition

ObservationDescribe visible losses and consult a condition report. A fragment cannot be treated as an intact original composition.
S08-E1

Age

Historical claimUse dated inscriptions or documented analysis. Cleveland’s fragment shows why a convincing style can still require revised dating.
S04-E1S07-E1

Provenance

Historical claimProvenance means documented ownership history. Retain gaps and questioned transfers.

Limit of this guide
