先觀察，再解釋Observe before interpreting

繁體中文

觀察 Observation：描述看得見的形狀。

歷史主張 Historical claim：回查年代、材質與產地紀錄。

詮釋 Interpretation：說明由誰提出、依據是什麼。

不確定 Uncertain：證據不足，就不下結論。

English

Observation: describe visible shapes.

Historical claim: check records for dates, materials and origin.

Interpretation: identify who proposed it and the supporting evidence.

Uncertain: do not draw a conclusion when evidence is insufficient.

這四類是本專案的編輯規則，不是地毯本身的分類。
These four categories are this project’s editorial rules, not classifications of rugs.

English

The field (matn / متن) is the main area inside the borders.

Main borders, minor borders and narrow guard bands help locate a motif.

Describe where it is before describing what it resembles.

歷史背景不等於固定象徵Historical context is not a fixed meaning

繁體中文

V&A 對 Ardabil 地毯大小不同的燈，介紹了透視效果與刻意不完美等解釋。

它同時指出透視說的證據問題，也沒有列出個別提出者。

因此應說「館方介紹一種解釋」，而不是「織者一定想表達……」。

English

The V&A discusses perspective and deliberate imperfection as possible explanations for the Ardabil Carpet’s unequal lamps.

It also questions the evidence for perspective and does not name the individual proponents.

Say “the museum presents an interpretation,” rather than claiming certainty about the weaver’s intention.

本 Wiki 不把任何紋樣解讀成唯一、普遍的寓意，也不加入虛構故事。
This Wiki assigns no universal meaning to a motif and contains no fictional stories.

保存、年代與來源紀錄Condition, age and provenance

繁體中文

保存狀況（condition）：是否有缺損、修補及檢查報告？

年代（age）：有銘文或分析支持嗎？Cleveland 的一件殘片經染料分析後，改定為 1884–1928 年。

來源紀錄（provenance）：誰曾擁有？不確定的轉手與年份要保留。

English

Condition: are losses, repairs and examination reports documented?

Age: is it supported by an inscription or analysis? Dye analysis led Cleveland to date one fragment to 1884–1928.

Provenance: who owned it? Keep uncertain transfers and dates visible.

紋樣本身不能決定價值。外觀古老也不等於年代已獲證明。
A motif alone does not determine value. An old-looking appearance does not establish age.

English

Show evidence layers

English

Acquire public sources; create evidence notes; organize a linked Wiki; check claims and image rights; approve the outline; translate and lay out slides; test the HTML.

Insufficient citation returns to sources. Unclear terminology returns to the Wiki. Layout or link failures are repaired and retested.

同一主張三次修訂仍無法支持，就標記未解決並交由人工檢查。
After three unsuccessful revisions of a claim, mark it unresolved and refer it for human review.
